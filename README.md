### [TruthSplit — Data Reconciliation & Root-Cause Analysis Platform](https://github.com/saxipatel/truthsplit)

A data reconciliation platform designed to investigate why two reports of the same business metric produce different results.

TruthSplit compares data from CSV files, Excel spreadsheets, and PostgreSQL queries, identifies discrepancies, and helps explain their underlying causes using evidence-based analysis.

**Key Features**
- Reconciliation across CSV, Excel, and SQL-based reports
- Evidence-based root-cause analysis and discrepancy attribution
- Detection of issues involving duplicates, refund policies, and reporting cutoffs
- What-if simulations to evaluate potential corrections
- Investigation history and audit tracking
- Automated backend and end-to-end testing
- Docker-based deployment and GitHub Actions CI

**Technologies:** Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React, TypeScript, Docker, GitHub Actions

**Testing:** 330 passing backend tests and 20 passing end-to-end tests.

**What I Learned:** How reconciliation systems investigate conflicting business metrics, trace discrepancies to potential causes, and validate results through automated testing.
